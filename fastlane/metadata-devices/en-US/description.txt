Aerilo is one button for a lighter photo library.

Tap Breathe. Aerilo looks through your photos and videos and gathers the ones you probably don't need. You review them, tick or untick, and decide what goes.

WHAT IT FINDS
• Exact duplicates: the same picture saved more than once. Aerilo keeps one of each.
• Similar shots: pictures taken seconds apart that look nearly the same. Aerilo suggests the best one to keep.
• Old screenshots: screenshots older than 30 days.
• Largest videos: the ones taking the most space. None are ticked for you.

YOU STAY IN CHARGE
• Nothing is deleted until you review it and tap Delete, and the system asks you to confirm.
• Deleted items stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days, so you can bring them back.
• While it works, Aerilo says what it is doing, step by step.

PRIVATE
• Photos are compared on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
• No account, no analytics, no ads.

A RECORD YOU CAN SHARE
Everything Aerilo looked at and everything you asked it to delete is written to a log on your device. Share it with support or keep it as a record.

ALSO IN THE APP
A Device screen with free storage, memory in use, processor load, temperature, battery and Low Power Mode.

GOOD TO KNOW
Aerilo for iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Apple Vision Pro tidies photos and storage. It does not speed up your device or manage other apps; these systems do that by themselves.

Aerilo is free and open source.
